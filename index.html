MY Denver Card Wallet

One card holds three funding streams: CCAP for childcare, MySpark for out-of-school activities, and SGO scholarship funds for education costs. Open any provider to see what your card covers there. For activities, get a one-time card number and register on the provider's own site. For childcare, send the provider a payment with a note saying exactly what it covers.

Try it

Jump to a screen

Each provider has its own profile. It shows which funding streams work there, what's left, and what that covers for each program or care period.

Three funding streams

What's on the card

CCAPChildcare subsidy. Monthly authorization for licensed childcare providers.
MySparkOut-of-school dollars for approved after-school, summer and enrichment programs.
SGO fundsScholarship dollars for education costs such as tuition, tutoring and school fees.

What changed in v4

  • Temporary cards for activities and education. "Pay with card" on a program creates a one-time card number locked to that provider and amount. Tap the card to show the number, then open the provider's registration page and pay at checkout.
  • Lump-sum payments for childcare. "Send a payment" goes straight to the childcare provider from CCAP. The family picks what it's for and writes exactly what it covers.
  • Funds are held when a card is created and returned if the card is canceled. Use the demo button to see the provider charge the card.
  • Still from v3: three funding streams with CCAP shown first, and a profile for every provider.
